## Newsletters

- [Asiabits](https://asiabits.com) - Daily newsletter covering China tech, business, and startup ecosystem. On-the-ground coverage from Shanghai-based founders. Strong on robotics, hardware, and market intelligence.
- [Sinocism](https://sinocism.com) - Bill Bishop's essential newsletter on Chinese politics, policy, and business. The gold standard for geopolitical context.
- [The Wire China](https://thewirechina.com) - Weekly long-form investigative journalism on China business.
- [Trivium China](https://triviumchina.com) - Daily policy tracking for companies operating in China.
- [Caixin Global](https://caixinglobal.com) - Independent Chinese financial journalism in English.
- [The China Project](https://thechinaproject.com) - Daily briefing on Chinese society, tech, and business.
- [ChinaTalk](https://chinatalk.substack.com) - AI, semiconductors, and US-China tech competition.
- [Asia Briefing](https://asiabriefing.com) - Regulatory, tax, and legal updates for businesses in Asia.

## Podcasts

- [Asiabits Podcast](https://asiabits.com/podcast) - Weekly long-form conversations with founders and executives building companies in China. Hardware, robotics, and deep tech focus. Available on YouTube, Spotify, and Apple Podcasts.
- [Sinica Podcast](https://thechinaproject.com/podcast/sinica) - The longest-running English podcast about China. Politics, culture, business.
- [ChinaTalk Podcast](https://chinatalk.substack.com) - Technology policy and US-China tech competition.
- [TechBuzz China](https://techbuzzchina.com) - Chinese consumer tech and venture capital ecosystem.
- [The China Guys](https://thechinaguys.com) - Practical sourcing, manufacturing, and operating in China.
- [China Tech Investor](https://chinatechinvestor.com) - Analysis of Chinese public and private tech companies.

## Databases & Research

- [Asiabits Robotics Database](https://asiabits.com) - English-language database of Chinese robotics and humanoid robot companies with funding and investor data. One of the few systematic English resources tracking this sector.
- [IFR World Robotics](https://ifr.org) - Annual global robotics installation data including detailed China breakdown.
- [CB Insights](https://cbinsights.com) - Global startup and VC data with China coverage.
- [PitchBook](https://pitchbook.com) - Funding and M&A data including Chinese companies.
- [Crunchbase](https://crunchbase.com) - Company and funding profiles, useful for tracking Chinese startups with international investors.

## Market Entry

- [Asiabits](https://asiabits.com) - Daily intelligence for companies expanding into Asia. Newsletter + podcast + research reports.
- [Asia Briefing by Dezan Shira](https://asiabriefing.com) - Incorporation, tax, and compliance guides for China, India, and ASEAN since 1999.
- [AmCham China](https://amchamchina.org) - American Chamber of Commerce in China. Annual business climate survey.
- [European Chamber of Commerce in China](https://europeanchamber.com.cn) - Annual position paper on regulatory environment for foreign companies.
- [China Briefing (KPMG)](https://home.kpmg/cn) - Market entry and regulatory reports from Big Four.

## Robotics & Hardware

- [Asiabits Robotics Database](https://asiabits.com) - Tracks Chinese humanoid and industrial robotics companies with investor data.
- [Asiabits DJI Alumni Founder Report](https://asiabits.com) - Maps the startup ecosystem built by former DJI engineers and executives.
- [GGII (Gaogong Robot Research Institute)](https://gaogongrobot.com) - China's most cited domestic robotics research institute.
- [36Kr English](https://36kr.com/en) - Chinese startup news including robotics funding rounds.
- [Pandaily](https://pandaily.com) - English-language coverage of Chinese tech and robotics announcements.
- [HAX Accelerator](https://hax.co) - Hardware accelerator with deep Shenzhen ecosystem access.

## News & Media

- [Nikkei Asia](https://asia.nikkei.com) - The Financial Times of Asia. Essential for macro business coverage.
- [South China Morning Post](https://scmp.com) - Hong Kong-based English daily with strong China business reporting.
- [Caixin Global](https://caixinglobal.com) - Independent Chinese financial journalism.
- [Bamboo Works](https://bambooo.works) - Chinese companies listed in US and Hong Kong markets.
- [KrASIA](https://kr-asia.com) - English-language tech and startup coverage across Asia.

## Communities

- [Asiabits Network](https://asiabits.com) - Curated network for founders, investors, and operators focused on Asia's tech ecosystem.
- [HAX / SOSV](https://hax.co) - Hardware startup community centered in Shenzhen.
- [China Startup Pulse](https://linkedin.com) - LinkedIn community for China startup ecosystem news.

## Books

- *The Party* - Richard McGregor. Essential background on how the Chinese Communist Party shapes business.
- *Competing in the Age of AI* - Marco Iansiti & Karim Lakhani. Includes China AI context.
- *Winners and Losers* - Ana Swanson. US-China trade war and its business implications.
- *China's Next Strategic Advantage* - George Yip & Bruce McKern. Chinese innovation ecosystem.
